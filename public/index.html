Stop running jobs from memory and group texts.

Every build is a hundred moving parts — inspections, draws, selections, subs. Benchmark keeps them in one place, in the order you actually build, so nothing slips between phases.

Every job, one list

A dashboard that tells you where to look first

Open Benchmark and see every active build — its stage, contract value, progress, and last activity. A quick status note keeps the story straight for whoever opens it next.

  • Live progress bar and current phase on every job
  • Freshness color flags the jobs that have gone quiet
  • Active and completed builds, kept separate
benchmarkpms.app / dashboard
Phase by phase

Built in phases, tracked by benchmark

Each job is broken into the phases you actually build — with the inspections, procurement, and sign-offs already in place. Check off a benchmark and the phase progress moves itself.

  • Inspection and procurement items flagged automatically
  • Completion dates, notes, and job-site photos per item
  • Reorder, rename, or move items between phases
benchmarkpms.app / project
Client selections

Let clients choose their finishes — live

Send a link. Your client picks paint, roof color, cabinet hardware, flooring and more from their phone — no login, no printed forms. You watch the choices come in as they make them.

  • Build your own selection lists, reuse them per job
  • Notes and reference photos on any choice
  • A running count of what's decided and what's left

The money, on track

Draws and change orders that add up

Tie a draw schedule to your phases and Benchmark reconciles it against the contract. Write up a change order, get it signed, and the contract total updates — original and current, side by side.

  • Draw schedule checked against the contract total
  • Change orders signed online, locked once accepted
  • Internal notes kept separate from what clients see

Keep clients in the loop

A progress link you're glad to share

Give clients and interested parties a clean, read-only view of their build — phases, progress, and photos, with none of your costs. One link, always current, no app to download.

  • Read-only — no financials, draws, or internal notes
  • Updates itself as the job moves
  • Turn any link off the moment you want to
